# Gamepass descriptions

Copy each description into the matching gamepass's Description field.

## 2x Display Coins

Earn 2x the base Coins from your Display, online and offline! Your highest owned Coin multiplier applies; Coin passes do not stack.

## 4x Display Coins

Earn 4x the base Coins from your Display, online and offline! Your highest owned Coin multiplier applies; Coin passes do not stack. Upgrades are full-price purchases, with no credit from earlier passes.

## 8x Display Coins

Earn 8x the base Coins from your Display, online and offline! Your highest owned Coin multiplier applies; Coin passes do not stack. Upgrades are full-price purchases, with no credit from earlier passes.

## Starter Pack

Start your collection with 7,500 Coins and one saved 15-minute 2x Display Coins boost token! Activate the token when you are ready; its timer runs continuously once activated. Rewards can be claimed once per account.

## Auto Open

Open blind boxes one at a time automatically using your Coins! Choose your collection, opening count and Coin reserve, then start or stop your run. Boxes keep their normal Coin price, with at least 3 seconds between openings.

## Auto Collect

Automatically collect your Display Coins every second while you are online! Switch Auto Collect on or off whenever you like. This collects your earnings without increasing your earning rate.

## Movement Upgrade

Get around Blindbox Town faster with an optional movement speed upgrade! Switch between boosted and normal speed whenever you like.

## VIP

Enjoy a permanent 10% bonus to Display Coin earnings, plus 500 bonus Coins with each daily Grove claim! Claim your daily reward to receive the extra Coins.

## Permanent Luck

Get a permanent luck boost for blind boxes, increasing your chances of Rare-or-better figures! Exact odds are shown before opening. This boost does not guarantee a particular figure.
