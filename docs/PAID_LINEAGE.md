# Paid Coin lineage and policy

Purchase enablement was requested by the owner; the 2026-10-13 candidate enables all retained products following the checks in [enablement evidence](audits/2026-10-13/monetization/ENABLEMENT.md). It is not yet published. The owner explicitly retained the existing shared pity formula and counters; there is no separate earned/paid pity history.

Schema 20 adds an earned wallet component, earned-only figure quantities, banks and slot capacity, a conserved assisted reserve, and lifetime package/offline transfer counters. Protected package/offline credit remains an immutable cumulative promise. Transfer counters prevent replay or rejoin from crediting already transferred or spent value. Wallet overflow remains protected. Policy transitions park assisted wallet value while preserving the earned wallet and earned-bank route. Fractional banks retain both sources. No namespace reset, marker pruning or progress wipe is implemented.

PaidPolicy caches the native ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted boolean for 60 seconds, uses bounded backoff on failures, fails closed for malformed, expired or unknown results, and fences late replies against replacement joins. Paid Coin spending, Luck, boosts and Auto Open require an Allowed result. Deterministic movement and Auto Collect retain their ownership paths. Receipts conserve purchased value regardless of policy; future use is checked independently. Economic purchase eligibility rechecks current policy after metadata lookups and before prompting.

Migration preserves pre-monetization wallet, copies, banks and slots as earned because prior production paths never made protected paid credit spendable. Schema 20 is forward-only. Older readers reject it; rollback must keep compatible readers and receipt/credit recovery for schema 20 and sold IDs. Publish and restart old servers together after release authorization. Do not change namespaces or reset existing profiles.
